How to do your workout safely

  1. Warm up for 5–10 minutes before exercising.
  2. Start with a light weight and learn the correct technique.
  3. Use controlled movements instead of swinging weights.
  4. Try 2–3 sets per exercise and rest 60–120 seconds between sets.
  5. Breathe continuously; do not hold your breath during repetitions.
  6. Stop if you feel sharp pain. Ask a qualified trainer for help with your form.
